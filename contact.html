Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about staying with Genesis

How do I book a room?

Booking is easy! Go to our Booking page, choose your hotel, dates and room type, and confirm. You’ll get instant confirmation by email / SMS. You can also call our 24/7 front desk.

What types of rooms do you have?

We offer Standard, Deluxe, Executive and Family rooms, plus Boutique Suites for longer stays. All rooms include free Wi-Fi, breakfast options and 24-hour housekeeping.

How are payments processed?

We handle all payments securely. Guests pay when they book, with instant confirmation. We accept major cards, mobile money and bank transfer for corporate bookings.

What safety measures do you have in place?

Safety is our top priority. All hotels have 24-hour reception, secure parking, CCTV and trained staff. We also have clear safety guidelines for events and conferences.

How do I find a hotel near me?

Visit our Hotels page and choose Kampala, Entebbe or Jinja. You can see photos, room types, rates and location — and book direct for the best rate.

Do you host conferences and weddings?

Yes! Our Conference & Events venues host meetings, weddings and corporate events for up to 300 guests, with catering, AV and event planning support.
